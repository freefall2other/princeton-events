Verdicts and favorites save automatically in this browser, on this device only until the shared sheet is added. Sources are grouped into sections: Favorites first, then each subject category. Click a star to add or remove a favorite — favorites keep the orange edge and move into the Favorites section, with the new grouping applied the next time the list is drawn (reload, or change a filter). Pick a category from Show to see just that category, favorites included and listed first. The grouping box is ticked to start with, tucking each family's subgroups under its parent — Reel Rebels and Global Cinema under the Garden Theatre, say; untick it to see every source as its own row. Subgroups keep their own stars: star one and the whole family moves into Favorites, with the parent showing a hollow gold star to say it is there on a subgroup's account rather than its own. Starring a parent never stars its subgroups. A subgroup filed under a different category than its parent still appears on its own when you pick that category. Sorting by any other column flattens the sections. Events is the upcoming count seen on the date in Last checked, with "~" an estimate and "+" a floor where the source hides its total; open Details on a row for the full wording, date range, filter and notes.
