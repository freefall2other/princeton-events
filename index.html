Verdicts and favorites save automatically in this browser, on this device only until the shared sheet is added. Cards and Logo, at the left of the toolbar, show the same sources two ways: Cards for browsing, and Logo for just the logos — click one to open that source's events page, or hover over it for its name; the layout you pick is remembered in this browser. Cards show each organisation's full logo; the few sources that have only a small mark show that instead. Sources are grouped into sections: Favorites first, then each category, each in alphabetical order — except that the central events calendar and University Ticketing always lead Favorites. Click a star to add or remove a favorite — favorites keep the orange edge and move into the Favorites section, with the new grouping applied the next time the list is drawn (reload, or change a filter). Pick a category from Show to see just that category, favorites included and listed first. The search box filters as you type and matches titles only — a source's name with the abbreviation shown beside it, and a subgroup's name through the family row that carries it, so "CITP" finds the Center for Information Technology Policy and "Tanner Lectures on Human Values" finds the University Center for Human Values. Nothing else is searched: a word that appears only in a subject or a note will not pull a row in. The count beside the box says how many sources match, and Escape empties it. Each section heading carries a box of its own on the right, which narrows that section and hides the others; a section searched down to nothing keeps its heading so the box is still there to clear. Locations works the same way, over room and building names, with a box in the toolbar and one in every section heading; star any room or building there to add it to the Favorites section at the top of Locations. Each family's subgroups are listed under its parent — Reel Rebels and Global Cinema under the Garden Theatre, say. Subgroups keep their own stars: star one and the whole family moves into Favorites, with the parent showing a hollow gold star to say it is there on a subgroup's account rather than its own. Starring a parent never stars its subgroups. A subgroup filed under a different category than its parent still appears on its own when you pick that category. Events is the upcoming count seen on the date in Last checked, with "~" an estimate and "+" a floor where the source hides its total.
