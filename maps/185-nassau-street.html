Two rooms here carry events rather than teaching: the James Stewart Film Theater (110) and the Hagan Gallery (120), both on floor 1 and both marked in orange on that plan. Floor 2 adds the Lucas Gallery, the Katzenbach Seminar Room (207) and a lecture room (219). Entrances are marked on floor 1, off the front and back courtyards; the Williams Street parking lot sits behind the building and Nassau Street in front. Each plan below opens full size in a new tab.
